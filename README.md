This entry writes a two-line warning to my past self: when Dad calls from the hospital at 2 a.m. and says "come now," don't tell him you'll come in the morning — because morning came too late, and the silence after that call taught me never to let a goodbye wait for a better hour. The program outputs exactly those two lines to entry.txt for make.fun's "Two Lines to Your Past Self" bounty.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
